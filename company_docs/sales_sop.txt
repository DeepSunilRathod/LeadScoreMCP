SALES STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

1. LEAD RESPONSE TIME
All new leads must be contacted within 30 minutes of assignment during business hours (9 AM - 7 PM IST).

2. FOLLOW-UP CADENCE
- Day 1: Initial call
- Day 3: Follow-up call if no response
- Day 7: WhatsApp message with product info
- Day 14: Final follow-up email before marking as cold

3. QUALIFYING QUESTIONS
Every sales call must cover:
- What CRM/tool are they currently using, if any?
- What is their team size?
- What is their monthly lead volume?
- What is their budget range?
- What is their decision timeline?

4. OBJECTION HANDLING
- "Too expensive": Pivot to ROI and time saved, not just price
- "Already have a CRM": Ask what's missing from their current solution
- "Need to think about it": Offer a free trial or demo instead of pushing for immediate close

5. ESCALATION
Leads with deal size above ₹50,000/year must be escalated to the Sales Manager before finalizing pricing.
